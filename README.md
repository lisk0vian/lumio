# Lumio

## What is Lumio?
Lumio is a modern interactive web application designed to calculate and break down electricity utility bills in Peru. It translates electrical consumption measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh) into Peruvian Soles (S/), providing precise transparency over energy costs, fixed charges, and taxes.

## Why "Lumio"?
The name **Lumio** is inspired by illumination, light, and clarity. It reflects the application's core mission: bringing absolute transparency and crystal-clear understanding to complex household and commercial electricity bills.

## Electricity Calculation Logic & Methodology
Lumio simplifies utility tariff computations through a structured breakdown:

1. **kWh to Soles Conversion:**
   - Multiplies the total active energy consumption (in kilowatt-hours) by the applicable tariff rate per kWh in Peruvian Soles (S/).

2. **Fixed Charges & Maintenance:**
   - Incorporates monthly fixed charges (cargo fijo), metering maintenance fees, and public electrical lighting contributions (alumbrado público) as mandated by local Peruvian regulatory frameworks.

3. **Subtotals & Taxes (IGV):**
   - Computes the net subtotal before taxes.
   - Applies the General Sales Tax (**IGV** - *Impuesto General a las Ventas*, currently 18% in Peru) when activated, ensuring users can toggle between tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive views.

4. **Historical Records & Tracking:**
   - Maintains a historical log comparing past kWh consumption against total monetary amounts in Soles, allowing users to analyze consumption trends over time.
